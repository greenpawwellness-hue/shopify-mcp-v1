Before you hit post

From the master brand brief

Never in customer-facing copy

curetreathealdiagnoseprevent diseaseclinically provenFDA approved
Your Key Benefits template already gets this right — "supports easier daily movement", not "fixes stiffness". Keep that wording when you copy it to a new product.

Say it this way instead

may supporthas been studied fortraditionally used tomany pet parents reportsupports a normal response
Soft benefit language, every time. It also sounds more like you than a claim does.

Words the brand doesn't use

poochpupperdoggofur kidfeline friendcompanion animalcanine companionpet guardian
Use: pup, fur baby, kitty, pet parent, dog mom, cat dad, the human — and the pack for the community.

No artificial ingredients — everNo harmful fillers
